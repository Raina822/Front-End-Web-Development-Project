Fun Facts

Learn some interesting things about Colorado!

  1. The mountainous area of Colorado is six times the size of Switzerland and contains 9,600 miles of fishing streams and over 1,000 peaks that are at least two miles high.

  2. Glenwood Springs houses the world's largest natural hot springs swimming pool.

  3. The highest paved road in North America is the road to Mount Evans, which climbs up to 14,258 feet above sea level.

  4. Leadville is the highest incorporated city in the U.S. at 10,430 feet above sea level.

  5. The Royal Gorge Bridge near Canon City is one of the highest suspension bridges in the world, at a height of 1,053 feet.

  6. Colfax Avenue in Denver is the longest continuous street in America.

  7. Denver has the largest urban park system in the nation.

Source from CCU
