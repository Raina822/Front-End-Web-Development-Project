Pueblo

Picture taken of the Pueblo Riverwalk at night.

Pueblo is a city in Colorado that is known for its steel industry.
The city is located in the southern part of the state and is home to the Colorado State Fair.

  • Population: 168,135 as of 2022
  • Classification: Urban
  • Average income: $59,436, which is lower than Colorado's average
  • Region: Southern Colorado
  • Year incorporated: 1870
(Pueblo, CO n.d)
