Colorado Springs

Picture taken of the winding roads up Pikes Peak.

Colorado Springs is a city in Colorado that is known for its military presence.
The city is located in the southern part of the state and is home to the United States Air Force Academy.

  • Population: 478,961 as of the 2020 census
  • Classification: Urban
  • Average income: $79,000, which is higher than Colorado's median household income.
  • Region: Located east of the Rocky Mountains
  • Year incorporated: 1871
