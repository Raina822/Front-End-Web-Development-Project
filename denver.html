Denver

Picture taken of the Denver skyline at sunset.

Denver is the capital of Colorado and is known for its outdoor activities.
The city is located in the northern part of the state and is home to the Denver Broncos.

  • Population: 710,800 as of 2022
  • Classification: Urban
  • Average income: $85,853, which is higher than Colorado's median household income.
  • Region: Northern Colorado
  • Year incorporated: 1858
